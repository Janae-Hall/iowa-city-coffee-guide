Pick your kind of night

Not every late coffee run is the same.

“I need to lock in.”

Night Owl is the fictional pick for headphones on, laptop open, and getting through the assignment before midnight.

Afterglow offers a more relaxed fictional evening atmosphere when coffee is part of the night rather than the entire reason for it.

The late-night rule

Choose the place that stays open longer than your motivation.

For Iowa City students, evening coffee can be less about the perfect latte and more about finding somewhere comfortable to finish what needs to get done. That's why this fictional guide puts closing time, study atmosphere, and purpose front and center.
