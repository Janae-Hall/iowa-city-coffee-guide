Built for Iowa City days

Coffee for the space between everything else.

The Daily Pour is made around the rhythms of a college town: grabbing caffeine before class, finding a table when the library feels too quiet, meeting a friend downtown, or settling in when the assignment is taking much longer than planned.

Before class: quick coffee, minimal waiting.

Study mode: tables, outlets, and room to stay.

Downtown afternoon: atmosphere matters.

After dark: somewhere that isn't closing yet.

The Daily Pour Edit

Three spots for three very different coffee days.

The slow morning pick

Juniper & Oak

Stay for the second cup.

Warm wood, oversized windows, comfortable seating, and a fictional brown sugar oat latte worth building a morning around.

Best for: Slow mornings & solo work
Vibe: Warm, calm, settled
Order: Brown sugar oat latte
The lock-in pick

Prairie Moon Coffee Co.

Laptop open. Phone down.

Big tables, plentiful outlets, and enough background noise to keep the fictional café lively without wrecking your focus.

Best for: Study sessions
Vibe: Productive, casual, social
Order: Honey cinnamon cold brew
The late-night pick

Night Owl Coffee Room

For the “one more assignment” crowd.

Individual lamps, outlets at nearly every seat, and a fictional midnight closing time make this the place for evenings that refuse to end.

Best for: Late-night studying
Vibe: Quiet after 8
Hours: Fictionally open until midnight
