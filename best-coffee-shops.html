01 · Best Overall

Juniper & Oak Coffee House

The place for mornings that don't need to be rushed.

Juniper & Oak is imagined as a warm, relaxed Iowa City café with oversized windows, natural wood, soft background music, and enough room to stay awhile. It's the fictional spot we'd pick when you want your coffee run to become part of the day instead of another thing to check off the list.

Laptop open. Phone down. Let's get something done.
